A helpful first visit

Arrive prepared and make the most of your time.

Whether you are asking about admission or simply learning more about the college, a little preparation can help you get the answers you need.

  • Confirm the campus location and current office hours.
  • Ask whether appointments or visitor registration are required.
  • Bring questions about courses, requirements, and costs.
  • Check for updates before traveling to campus.
